The Matilda Timeline

These extraordinary women scientists whose contributions have been minimized or attributed to others

1815–1852

Ada Lovelace

First computer algorithm

Considered the first programmer in history, she wrote the first algorithm intended to be executed by a machine.

1878–1968

Lise Meitner

Nuclear fission

An Austro-Swedish physicist, she discovered nuclear fission but the Nobel Prize was awarded only to her colleague Otto Hahn.

1900–1979

Cecilia Payne-Gaposchkin

Composition of stars

A British-American astronomer, she discovered that stars are mainly composed of hydrogen and helium.

1912–1997

Chien-Shiung Wu

Parity in physics

A Chinese-American physicist, she conducted the experiment demonstrating parity violation, but only her male colleagues received the Nobel Prize.

1920–1958

Rosalind Franklin

DNA Structure (Photo 51)

Her X-ray crystallography work was essential to discovering the structure of DNA.

1861–1912

Nettie Stevens

Sex chromosomes

An American geneticist, she discovered that sex is determined by chromosomes (X and Y).

1943–

Jocelyn Bell Burnell

Discovery of pulsars

A British astrophysicist, she discovered pulsars during her thesis, but the Nobel Prize was awarded to her thesis supervisor.
