MMI Project

This website is a student project created as part of the BUT Métiers du Multimédia et de l'Internet (MMI) program. It explores the life and work of Rosalind Franklin, a British crystallographer whose work was crucial in discovering the structure of DNA.

Why Rosalind Franklin?

Her story perfectly illustrates the Matilda Effect: the minimization of women's scientific contributions. This project aims to honor her legacy and create bridges between science and popular culture.
