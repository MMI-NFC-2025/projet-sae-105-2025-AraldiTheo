The Matilda Effect

The Matilda Effect refers to the systematic denial or minimization of women scientists' contributions to research. Named after Matilda Joslyn Gage, this phenomenon has long rendered invisible major discoveries made by women.

This site aims to do justice to these pioneers and to highlight their fundamental work.
